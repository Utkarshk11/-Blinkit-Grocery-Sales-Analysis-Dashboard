🔍 **My Approach – STAR Method**

🔹 **Situation | Understanding the Business**

Retail businesses generate data across products, outlet locations, outlet sizes, product characteristics, and customer ratings. However, raw data alone doesn't provide an actionable view of performance.

The challenge was to organize this information into a meaningful analytical dashboard.

🔹 **Task | Defining the Analytical Objective**

I aimed to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that would:

• Monitor overall sales performance through KPIs.  
• Identify high-performing product categories.  
• Compare sales across outlet types and locations.  
• Analyze sales distribution based on outlet size and tier.  
• Explore sales trends across outlet establishment years.  
• Evaluate the contribution of Low Fat and Regular Fat products.

🔹 **Action | Data Analysis & Dashboard Development**

To achieve these objectives, I worked on:

✅ Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.  
✅ Developing KPI indicators for sales performance monitoring.  
✅ Building interactive visualizations for product-level analysis.  
✅ Analyzing outlet performance across different outlet types and tiers.  
✅ Comparing sales based on outlet size and fat content.  
✅ Exploring outlet establishment trends and sales performance.  
✅ Implementing interactive slicers and filters for dynamic analysis.  
✅ Designing a structured dashboard to improve data readability and analytical exploration.

🔹 **Result | Turning Data into Business Insights**

📌 Consolidated **$1.20M in sales performance** into an interactive dashboard.

📌 Analyzed **8,523 items** to explore product-level sales distribution.

📌 Evaluated outlet performance across multiple outlet segments to identify sales patterns and compare business performance.

📌 Converted key business indicators into an interactive reporting solution that simplifies performance monitoring and exploratory analysis.

📌 Created a single dashboard experience that allows users to explore sales performance across multiple business dimensions rather than relying on static reports.
